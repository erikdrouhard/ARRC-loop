# FAQ

## Why not just use a regular judge?

Because a regular judge usually checks quality once. ARRC Loop checks whether quality survives structured challenge under protocol constraints.

## Is ARRC Loop just debate?

No. Debate is one ingredient. ARRC Loop is a relay-mediated convergence protocol that uses structured disagreement to reach a justified termination state.

## Is ARRC Loop just self-refine?

No. Self-refine is primarily about improving the artifact. ARRC Loop is about determining whether the artifact has reached a justified termination state under adequate challenge.

## Should every response go through ARRC Loop?

No. Low-stakes or latency-sensitive outputs often deserve a bypass or a light screen. The tier system (0–3) handles this routing.

## What does ARRC Loop reward?

It rewards correct resistance and correct revision.

That means:

- resisting weak criticism
- updating for valid criticism
- narrowing claims when certainty is too high
- escalating when the system should not bluff

## Does ARRC Loop replace human review?

No. It is a convergence protocol, not a guarantee of truth. The resolver has workflow authority, not truth authority.

## What counts as a good ARRC outcome?

There are three good outcomes, depending on the case:

- approve because the draft holds under adequate challenge
- narrow because the draft was directionally correct but overstated
- escalate because the system should stop pretending certainty

## What is the core invariant?

Stability without adequate challenge coverage is not convergence. A draft that stops changing because challenges were weak or absent is not approved — it is escalated.

## Why is relay a first-class role?

Because transport between modules is part of the protocol, not incidental plumbing. The relay enforces constraints: no invention, no omission, lineage preserved. Without it, modules might silently transform arguments in transit.

## Why "resolver" instead of "judge"?

The resolver determines whether the loop reached a justified termination state. It has workflow authority, not truth authority. Calling it a "judge" implies epistemic authority it does not have.
