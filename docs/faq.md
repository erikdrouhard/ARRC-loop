# FAQ

## Why not just use a regular judge?

Because a regular judge usually checks quality once. ARC Judge checks whether quality survives challenge.

## Is ARC Judge just debate?

No. Debate is one ingredient. ARC Judge is a decision layer that uses rebuttal pressure to make a calibrated handoff decision.

## Is ARC Judge just self-refine?

No. Self-refine is primarily about improving the artifact. ARC Judge is about deciding whether the artifact remains trustworthy after challenge.

## Should every response go through ARC Judge?

No. Low-stakes or latency-sensitive outputs often deserve a bypass or a light screen.

## What does ARC Judge reward?

It rewards correct resistance and correct revision.

That means:

- resisting weak criticism
- updating for valid criticism
- narrowing claims when certainty is too high
- escalating when the system should not bluff

## Does ARC Judge replace human review?

No. It is a calibration layer, not a guarantee of truth.

## What counts as a good ARC outcome?

There are three good outcomes, depending on the case:

- approve because the draft holds
- narrow because the draft was directionally correct but overstated
- escalate because the system should stop pretending certainty
